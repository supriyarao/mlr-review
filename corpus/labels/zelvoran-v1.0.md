# ZELVORAN (fictional) — Label extract, version 1.0

> Synthetic content for demonstration. Zelvoran is not a real medicine.

## 1. Indications and Usage

ZELVORAN is indicated for the treatment of moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis in adults who
are candidates for systemic therapy or phototherapy.

Limitations of use. The safety and effectiveness of ZELVORAN have not been established in
paediatric patients (under 18 years of age). ZELVORAN has not been studied in psoriatic arthritis,
guttate psoriasis, or pustular psoriasis. ZELVORAN has not been compared with any other biologic
therapy in a head-to-head trial.

## 2. Dosage and Administration

The recommended dosage is 180 mg administered by subcutaneous injection at Week 0 and Week 2,
followed by 180 mg every 4 weeks thereafter.

Evaluate patients for tuberculosis infection prior to initiating treatment. Do not administer to
patients with active tuberculosis.

## 3. Contraindications

- Active, serious infection, including active tuberculosis.
- Known serious hypersensitivity to zelvoran or any excipient.

## 4. Warnings and Precautions

Serious infections. ZELVORAN may increase the risk of serious bacterial, fungal and viral
infections. Do not initiate during an active serious infection. Interrupt treatment if a serious
infection develops.

Tuberculosis. Screen for latent tuberculosis before initiating treatment and consider
anti-tuberculosis therapy before starting ZELVORAN in patients with latent infection.

Hepatic enzyme elevations. Elevations in ALT or AST greater than three times the upper limit of
normal occurred in 2.1% of ZELVORAN-treated patients compared with 0.6% receiving placebo. Monitor
liver enzymes at baseline and periodically during treatment.

Hypersensitivity reactions. Serious hypersensitivity reactions, including angioedema, have been
reported. Discontinue immediately if a serious reaction occurs.

Malignancy. The effect of ZELVORAN on the risk of malignancy is not known. No conclusion
about malignancy risk can be drawn from the clinical trial programme.

Immunisations. Avoid use of live vaccines during treatment.

## 5. Adverse Reactions

Adverse reactions occurring in at least 2% of ZELVORAN-treated patients and more frequently than
placebo during the 16-week placebo-controlled periods of ZEL-301 and ZEL-302:

| Adverse reaction | ZELVORAN 180 mg (n=779) | Placebo (n=777) |
|---|---|---|
| Nasopharyngitis | 14.1% | 11.3% |
| Injection-site reaction | 9.4% | 2.0% |
| Headache | 7.2% | 5.9% |
| Upper respiratory tract infection | 6.1% | 4.8% |
| Arthralgia | 3.3% | 2.6% |
| Elevated transaminases | 2.1% | 0.6% |

## 6. Clinical Studies

### 6.1 ZEL-301 — PASI-75 at Week 16

Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 812 adults with moderate-to-severe plaque
psoriasis. Co-primary endpoints at Week 16:

- PASI-75: 68.4% of ZELVORAN patients versus 9.2% of placebo patients.
- sPGA 0/1 (clear or almost clear): 61.0% versus 7.1%.

### 6.2 ZEL-302 — PASI-90 at Week 16, with a prespecified subgroup

Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 744 adults. Primary endpoint at
Week 16:

- PASI-90: 44.2% of ZELVORAN patients versus 4.8% of placebo patients.

A prespecified subgroup analysis by prior biologic exposure found:

| Subgroup | n | PASI-90 at Week 16 |
|---|---|---|
| Prior biologic exposure | 203 | 58.1% |
| No prior biologic exposure | 541 | 39.0% |

The subgroup result describes patients with prior biologic exposure only. It is not an estimate
of response in the overall population, which was 44.2%.

### 6.3 ZEL-303 — Maintenance of PASI-75 through Week 52

Open-label extension enrolling 610 adults who completed ZEL-301 or ZEL-302. Endpoint reported:

- PASI-75 at Week 16: 71.2%.
- PASI-75 maintained at Week 52: 64.8% of those who achieved it at Week 16.

ZEL-303 reports PASI-75, not PASI-90, and its timepoints are Week 16 and Week 52. ZEL-303
did not assess PASI-90 and did not report any Week 12 endpoint. No data beyond Week 52 exist for
ZELVORAN.

### 6.4 Studies not conducted

No head-to-head comparison against another biologic therapy has been conducted. No cardiovascular
outcomes trial has been conducted. No paediatric study has been conducted.
