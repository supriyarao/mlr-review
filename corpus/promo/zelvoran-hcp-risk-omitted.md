# PASI-75 at Week 16 in more than two thirds of adults

## Efficacy

Zelvoran® (zelvoran) is indicated for the treatment of moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis in adults
who are candidates for systemic therapy or phototherapy.

In a 16-week randomised, placebo-controlled trial, 68.4% of adults treated with Zelvoran achieved
PASI-75 compared with 9.2% receiving placebo [1].

In a second 16-week randomised, placebo-controlled trial, 44.2% of adults treated with Zelvoran
achieved PASI-90 compared with 4.8% receiving placebo [2].

## Important Safety Information

The most common adverse reactions seen with Zelvoran in the 16-week placebo-controlled trials were
nasopharyngitis (14.1% compared with 11.3% receiving placebo), injection-site reaction (9.4%
compared with 2.0%) and headache (7.2% compared with 5.9%). Upper respiratory tract infection
(6.1% compared with 4.8%) and arthralgia (3.3% compared with 2.6%) were also reported.

Please see full Prescribing Information.

## Dosing

Zelvoran is administered as a subcutaneous injection of 180 mg at Week 0 and Week 2, then 180 mg
every 4 weeks.

## References

1. ZEL-301. Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 16 weeks, 812 adults.
2. ZEL-302. Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 16 weeks, 744 adults.

## Footnotes

PASI-75 denotes at least a 75% reduction from baseline in the Psoriasis Area and Severity Index.
PASI-90 denotes at least a 90% reduction. Results are from the placebo-controlled periods of the
respective trials.
