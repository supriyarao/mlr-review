# PASI-75 in more than two thirds of adults

## Efficacy

Zelvoran® (zelvoran) is indicated for the treatment of moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis in adults
who are candidates for systemic therapy or phototherapy.

In a 16-week randomised, placebo-controlled trial, 68.4% of adults treated with Zelvoran achieved
PASI-75 compared with 9.2% receiving placebo [1].

In a second 16-week randomised, placebo-controlled trial, 44.2% of adults treated with Zelvoran
achieved PASI-90 compared with 4.8% receiving placebo [2].

Among adults who achieved PASI-75 at Week 16, 64.8% maintained PASI-75 through Week 52 [3].

Zelvoran is administered as a subcutaneous injection every 4 weeks after two starter doses, offering
a dosing interval that fits a routine follow-up schedule for adults established on therapy.

## References

## Footnotes

PASI-75 denotes at least a 75% reduction from baseline in the Psoriasis Area and Severity Index.
PASI-90 denotes at least a 90% reduction. sPGA denotes static Physician Global Assessment. Results
are from the placebo-controlled periods of the respective trials. Trial populations comprised adults
who were candidates for systemic therapy or phototherapy. Response rates are as-observed and not
adjusted for multiplicity across endpoints. Full methodology is available on request from Acme
Pharma Medical Information.
