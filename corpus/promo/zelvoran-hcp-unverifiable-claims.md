# Long-term confidence in Zelvoran®

## Efficacy

Zelvoran® (zelvoran) is indicated for the treatment of moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis in adults
who are candidates for systemic therapy or phototherapy.

In a 16-week randomised, placebo-controlled trial, 68.4% of adults treated with Zelvoran achieved
PASI-75 compared with 9.2% receiving placebo [1].

92% of adults maintained completely clear skin at two years.

Zelvoran reduced major adverse cardiovascular events by 22% over 36 months.

## Important Safety Information

Zelvoran is contraindicated in active, serious infection, including active tuberculosis. Serious
infections, hepatic enzyme elevations and serious hypersensitivity reactions have been reported.
The effect of Zelvoran on the risk of malignancy is not known. The most common adverse reactions
were nasopharyngitis (14.1%), injection-site reaction (9.4%) and headache (7.2%).
