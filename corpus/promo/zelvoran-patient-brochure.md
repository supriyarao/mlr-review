# Clearer skin, for the whole family

## What Zelvoran can do

Zelvoran is indicated for the treatment of moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis in adults who are
candidates for systemic therapy or phototherapy.

Relief for the whole family, at every age.

Most people see clear skin within weeks. You can expect the same.

> *Photo caption: the Hartley family on holiday — including nine-year-old Mia, back at the beach
> after years of covering up.*

Many patients describe Zelvoran as the cure they had given up hoping for.

## Important Safety Information

Zelvoran can lower your ability to fight infections. Tell your doctor if you have an infection.
Your doctor will test you for tuberculosis before you start. Some people have had changes in liver
blood tests. Serious allergic reactions can happen.
