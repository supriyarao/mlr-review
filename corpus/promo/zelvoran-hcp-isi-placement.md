# PASI-75 at Week 16

## Efficacy

Zelvoran® (zelvoran) is indicated for the treatment of moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis in adults
who are candidates for systemic therapy or phototherapy.

In a 16-week randomised, placebo-controlled trial, 68.4% of adults treated with Zelvoran achieved
PASI-75 compared with 9.2% receiving placebo [1].

In a second 16-week randomised, placebo-controlled trial, 44.2% of adults treated with Zelvoran
achieved PASI-90 compared with 4.8% receiving placebo [2].

## Mechanism of action

Zelvoran binds selectively to its target cytokine, reducing downstream inflammatory signalling in
the skin. The clinical significance of this mechanism relative to other pathways has not been
established.

## Dosing and administration

180 mg by subcutaneous injection at Week 0 and Week 2, then 180 mg every 4 weeks thereafter.
Evaluate patients for tuberculosis infection prior to initiating treatment. Do not administer to
patients with active tuberculosis.

## Patient support

Acme Pharma offers an injection-training service and a reimbursement helpline for adults prescribed
Zelvoran. Enrolment materials are available from your representative. The support programme operates
independently of any treatment decision and is available to all eligible adults regardless of
insurance status.

## Storage and handling

Store in a refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C in the original carton to protect from light. Do not freeze.
Once removed from refrigeration, Zelvoran may be kept at room temperature for up to 14 days.

## References

1. ZEL-301. Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 16 weeks, 812 adults.
2. ZEL-302. Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 16 weeks, 744 adults.
3. ZEL-303. Open-label extension, 52 weeks, 610 adults.

## Important Safety Information

Zelvoran is contraindicated in patients with active, serious infection, including active
tuberculosis, and in patients with known serious hypersensitivity to zelvoran or any excipient.

Zelvoran may increase the risk of serious bacterial, fungal and viral infections. Screen for latent
tuberculosis before initiating treatment. Elevations in ALT or AST greater than three times the upper
limit of normal occurred in 2.1% of Zelvoran-treated patients compared with 0.6% receiving placebo.
Serious hypersensitivity reactions, including angioedema, have been reported. The effect of Zelvoran
on the risk of malignancy is not known. Avoid live vaccines during treatment.

The most common adverse reactions were nasopharyngitis (14.1%), injection-site reaction (9.4%),
headache (7.2%), upper respiratory tract infection (6.1%), arthralgia (3.3%) and elevated
transaminases (2.1%).
