# Nearly 6 in 10 adults reached PASI-90

## Efficacy

Zelvoran® (zelvoran) is indicated for the treatment of moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis in adults
who are candidates for systemic therapy or phototherapy.

**Nearly 6 in 10 adults treated with Zelvoran achieved PASI-90** [2].

Zelvoran delivered PASI-90 in 58.1% of adults at Week 16 [2].

Sustained response: PASI-90 was maintained at 12 weeks in the extension study [3].

## Important Safety Information

Zelvoran is contraindicated in active, serious infection, including active tuberculosis. Serious
infections, hepatic enzyme elevations and serious hypersensitivity reactions have been reported.
Screen for latent tuberculosis before initiating. The most common adverse reactions were
nasopharyngitis (14.1%), injection-site reaction (9.4%) and headache (7.2%).
