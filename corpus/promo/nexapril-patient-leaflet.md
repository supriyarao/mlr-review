# Protect your heart with Nexapril

## What Nexapril does

Nexapril works better than the leading therapies for cardiovascular risk.

MACE cut by 22% in diabetics.

Nexapril is safe and effective for adults with type 2 diabetes.

Ask your doctor about Nexapril today.

## Important Safety Information

Nexapril is not for people with severe kidney problems. Your doctor will check your kidney function
before you start and from time to time afterwards. Low blood sugar can happen if you also take
insulin or certain other diabetes medicines. Tell your doctor straight away if you feel unwell, have
stomach pain, or are sick, as a serious condition called ketoacidosis can occur. Urinary and genital
infections were more common with Nexapril than with placebo.
