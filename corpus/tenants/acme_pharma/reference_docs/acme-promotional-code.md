# Acme Brand and Promotional Code

> Fictional internal code of practice, written for this demonstration. It is not based on, and does
> not reproduce, any real company's code or any regulatory authority's guidance.

Version 2026.1. Owner: Regulatory Affairs. Applies to all promotional material for Acme products.

## 1. Consistency with the label

All claims must be consistent with the approved label. Where a claim restates a labelled result, it
must preserve the population, the comparator, the endpoint and the timepoint of that result.

## 2. Subgroup results

A result observed in a subgroup must be described as a subgroup result, naming the subgroup. A
subgroup figure must never be presented as the result in the overall population.

## 3. Comparative claims

A claim that an Acme product is better than, more effective than, or preferable to another therapy
requires a head-to-head clinical trial against that named therapy. Comparative language without a
named comparator and a head-to-head reference is not permitted.

## 4. Safety information

Every promotional piece must carry the Important Safety Information for the product. Safety
information must be legible and must not be reduced in size or prominence relative to efficacy
content.

## 5. Durability of effect

Claims about how long an effect lasts must not extend beyond the longest timepoint for which data
exist.

## 6. Audience and population

Material must address only the population in the approved indication. Imagery, testimonials and
copy must not depict or imply use in a population outside that indication, including by depicting
children where the indication is limited to adults.

## 7. Trademarks

The product name must carry the registered trademark symbol on first mention in every piece.

## 8. References

Every claim of efficacy or safety must carry a reference to the specific study supporting it. The
cited study must report the endpoint, population and timepoint stated in the claim.

## 9. Prohibited language

Promotional material must not state or imply that an Acme product cures the condition it treats,
that a result is guaranteed, or that the product is completely safe or free of risk.

## 10. Patient-directed material

Patient-directed material must not imply that the results described are typical of all patients
unless the label supports that characterisation, and must direct the reader to a healthcare
professional.

## 11. Prescribing information

Every piece must direct the reader to the full Prescribing Information.

## 12. Review and approval

No promotional material may be distributed without the approval of a qualified reviewer. Approval is
recorded against the reviewer who gave it.
