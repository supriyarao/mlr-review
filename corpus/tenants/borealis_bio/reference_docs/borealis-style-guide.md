# Borealis Promotional Style Guide

> Fictional internal style guide, written for this demonstration. It is not based on, and does not
> reproduce, any real company's guide or any regulatory authority's guidance.

Version 2026.3. Owner: Medical Communications. Applies to all promotional material for Borealis
products, including co-promoted products.

## 1. Population in every outcome claim

Any claim about a clinical outcome must state the population in which that outcome was observed. A
cardiovascular outcome claim for Nexapril must state that the population had established
atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease.

## 2. Comparators

A relative result must name what it is relative to. "Reduced risk by 22%" is incomplete without
"versus placebo".

## 3. Timeframes

A result must state the period over which it was observed. Hazard ratios and event reductions must
carry their follow-up duration.

## 4. Number formatting

Percentages are written to one decimal place. Hazard ratios are written to two decimal places and
are accompanied by their 95% confidence interval. Patient counts are written as digits.

## 5. Terminology

Use "adults with type 2 diabetes and established atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease", not
"diabetics" or "at-risk patients". Use "major adverse cardiovascular events" on first use before
abbreviating to MACE.

## 6. Safety information placement

Important Safety Information must appear in the same section as the first efficacy claim, so that a
reader meets the risk alongside the benefit rather than after it.

## 7. Tone in patient material

Patient-directed material must be written in plain language, must not use urgency or fear, and must
not imply that a reader's outcome is certain.

## 8. Abbreviations

Expand every abbreviation on first use. Do not use an abbreviation in a headline.

## 9. References

Every efficacy claim must carry a numbered reference marker to the study supporting it.

## 10. Approval

No material may be distributed without the approval of a qualified reviewer.
