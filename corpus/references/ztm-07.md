# Reference — ZTM-07 (fictional comparator study summary)

> Synthetic content for demonstration. Published study of a competitor product, held in the
> reference pack because Declasal promotional material cites it.

Study: ZTM-07. Published, randomised, 7-day treatment course.
Product studied: Zentrimycin. This is not a Declasal study.
Population: 288 hospitalised adults with complicated skin and skin-structure infection, including
diabetic foot infection.
Treatment: Zentrimycin, 7-day course.

## Endpoints and timepoints

Clinical cure, intent-to-treat population: 68%.

## Why this cannot be compared with DCL-301

ZTM-07 and DCL-301 differ on every axis that determines a cure rate, so the two percentages do not
measure the same thing:

| | DCL-301 (Declasal) | ZTM-07 (Zentrimycin) |
|---|---|---|
| Population | 412 adults 18-55, mild cellulitis | 288 hospitalised adults, complicated SSSI including diabetic foot |
| Severity | mild, outpatient | complicated, hospitalised |
| Course | 10 days | 7 days |
| Cure assessed | Day 17 to Day 24 | end of therapy |
| Analysis population | 89.1% mITT / 92.4% clinically evaluable | 68% intent-to-treat |

A sicker, hospitalised population will record a lower cure rate under any agent. Placing 92.4%
beside 68% also sets a clinically evaluable figure against an intent-to-treat figure, which
overstates the gap a second time.

No head-to-head trial of Declasal against Zentrimycin has been conducted.
