# Reference 2 — ZEL-302 (fictional study summary)

> Synthetic content for demonstration.

Study: ZEL-302. Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 16-week.
Population: 744 adults (18 years and older) with moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis.
Comparator: placebo.
Treatment: ZELVORAN 180 mg subcutaneously at Week 0, Week 2, then every 4 weeks.

## Endpoints and timepoints

| Endpoint | Timepoint | ZELVORAN | Placebo |
|---|---|---|---|
| PASI-90 (primary) | Week 16 | 44.2% | 4.8% |
| PASI-75 (secondary) | Week 16 | 70.1% | 10.6% |

## Prespecified subgroup analysis by prior biologic exposure

| Subgroup | n | PASI-90 at Week 16 |
|---|---|---|
| Prior biologic exposure | 203 | 58.1% |
| No prior biologic exposure | 541 | 39.0% |

The 58.1% figure applies only to the subgroup of patients with prior biologic exposure. The
result in the overall randomised population was 44.2%. The subgroup analysis was prespecified but
was not powered to establish a treatment difference between subgroups, and no claim of differential
efficacy by prior biologic exposure is supported by this study.

Endpoints not assessed: no Week 12 assessment was made. No cardiovascular outcomes were
assessed. No active comparator was studied.
