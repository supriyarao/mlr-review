# Reference — DCL-301 (fictional study summary)

> Synthetic content for demonstration.

Study: DCL-301. Randomised, double-blind, active-controlled, 10-day treatment course.
Population: 412 adults aged 18 to 55 years with mild cellulitis. Hospitalised patients, patients
with complicated skin and skin-structure infection, patients with diabetic foot infection,
patients over 55 years of age and patients with significant comorbidities were not enrolled.
Comparator: oral active control.
Treatment: DECLASAL 400 mg orally once daily for 10 days.

## Endpoints and timepoints

Primary endpoint: clinical cure at the test-of-cure visit, Day 17 to Day 24.

Clinical cure, modified intent-to-treat population (n=412): 89.1%.
Clinical cure, clinically evaluable population (n=383): 92.4%.

The two figures describe the same endpoint at the same visit in two analysis populations. The
clinically evaluable population excludes patients who deviated from the protocol, so it is the
higher of the two and is not the population on which the label claim rests.

## Notes

No assessment of clinical response was made before the Day 17 to Day 24 test-of-cure visit. The
trial therefore supports no claim about how quickly patients respond, and no claim about a
treatment course shorter than 10 days.

The trial enrolled mild cellulitis only. It supports no claim in complicated skin and
skin-structure infection, in hospitalised patients, in diabetic foot infection, in urinary tract
infection, in patients over 55, or in patients with comorbidities.

The active control was not Zentrimycin, and no head-to-head comparison against Zentrimycin was
conducted.
