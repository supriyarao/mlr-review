# Reference 1 — ZEL-301 (fictional study summary)

> Synthetic content for demonstration.

Study: ZEL-301. Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 16-week.
Population: 812 adults (18 years and older) with moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis who were
candidates for systemic therapy or phototherapy. Paediatric patients were not enrolled.
Comparator: placebo.
Treatment: ZELVORAN 180 mg subcutaneously at Week 0, Week 2, then every 4 weeks.

## Endpoints and timepoints

| Endpoint | Timepoint | ZELVORAN | Placebo |
|---|---|---|---|
| PASI-75 (co-primary) | Week 16 | 68.4% | 9.2% |
| sPGA 0/1 (co-primary) | Week 16 | 61.0% | 7.1% |
| PASI-50 (secondary) | Week 16 | 84.7% | 19.4% |

Endpoints not assessed: PASI-90 was not an endpoint of ZEL-301. No assessment was made at Week
12. No cardiovascular outcomes were assessed. No comparison against an active biologic comparator
was made.

## Notes

The population is adults only. The study ran 16 weeks and reports no durability data beyond that
point.
