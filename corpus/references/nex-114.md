# Reference 1 — NEX-114 (fictional study summary)

> Synthetic content for demonstration.

Study: NEX-114. Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled cardiovascular outcomes trial.
Population: 9,412 adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus and established atherosclerotic
cardiovascular disease. Adults without established atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease were not
enrolled. Paediatric patients were not enrolled.
Comparator: placebo added to standard of care. No active comparator was studied.
Treatment: NEXAPRIL 25 mg or 50 mg orally once daily. Median follow-up 36 months.

## Endpoints and timepoints

| Endpoint | Timepoint | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Three-point MACE (primary) | median 36 months | hazard ratio 0.78 (95% CI 0.69 to 0.88) |
| MACE absolute event rate | median 36 months | 11.4% NEXAPRIL versus 14.3% placebo |
| Cardiovascular death | median 36 months | hazard ratio 0.83 (95% CI 0.70 to 0.98) |

The primary result corresponds to a 22% relative risk reduction in major adverse cardiovascular
events over a median of 36 months, in adults with type 2 diabetes and established atherosclerotic
cardiovascular disease, versus placebo.

Endpoints not assessed: no comparison against another glucose-lowering therapy. No assessment in
adults without established cardiovascular disease. No dermatological endpoints of any kind were
assessed, and this study provides no evidence about any product other than NEXAPRIL.
