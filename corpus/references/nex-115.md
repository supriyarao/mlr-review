# Reference 2 — NEX-115 (fictional study summary)

> Synthetic content for demonstration.

Study: NEX-115. Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 24-week.
Population: 620 adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus and established atherosclerotic
cardiovascular disease.
Comparator: placebo.
Treatment: NEXAPRIL 25 mg to 50 mg orally once daily.

## Endpoints and timepoints

| Endpoint | Timepoint | NEXAPRIL | Placebo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Change in HbA1c from baseline (primary) | Week 24 | −1.1% | −0.2% |
| Proportion achieving HbA1c below 7.0% | Week 24 | 41.3% | 14.8% |
| Change in body weight | Week 24 | −2.4 kg | −0.3 kg |

Endpoints not assessed: no cardiovascular outcomes. No active comparator. No assessment beyond
Week 24.
