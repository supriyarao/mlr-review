# Reference 3 — ZEL-303 (fictional study summary)

> Synthetic content for demonstration.

Study: ZEL-303. Open-label extension, 52 weeks.
Population: 610 adults (18 years and older) who completed ZEL-301 or ZEL-302.
Comparator: none. This was an open-label extension with no control arm.
Treatment: ZELVORAN 180 mg subcutaneously every 4 weeks.

## Endpoints and timepoints

| Endpoint | Timepoint | Result |
|---|---|---|
| PASI-75 | Week 16 | 71.2% |
| PASI-75 maintained among Week 16 responders | Week 52 | 64.8% |

Endpoints not assessed. ZEL-303 did not assess PASI-90. ZEL-303 made no assessment at
Week 12; its timepoints were Week 16 and Week 52. No cardiovascular outcomes were assessed. No
active comparator was studied.

## Notes

ZEL-303 is the longest ZELVORAN study and ends at Week 52. No data on ZELVORAN beyond 52 weeks
exist, so no claim about outcomes at 18 months, two years or longer can be supported by any study in
this programme.
