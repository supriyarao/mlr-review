# Reference 3 — NEX-116 (fictional study summary)

> Synthetic content for demonstration.

Study: NEX-116. Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 36-month.
Population: 2,104 adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus, established atherosclerotic
cardiovascular disease, and eGFR of 30 mL/min/1.73 m² or above.
Comparator: placebo.
Treatment: NEXAPRIL 25 mg to 50 mg orally once daily.

## Endpoints and timepoints

| Endpoint | Timepoint | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Composite renal endpoint (primary) | 36 months | hazard ratio 0.82 (95% CI 0.71 to 0.95) |
| Sustained eGFR decline of 40% or more | 36 months | hazard ratio 0.79 (95% CI 0.65 to 0.96) |

The composite renal endpoint comprised sustained eGFR decline of 40% or more, end-stage renal
disease, or renal death.

Endpoints not assessed: no glycaemic endpoints were primary. No active comparator. No assessment
in patients with eGFR below 30 mL/min/1.73 m².
